🇮🇳 Spice Junction - Indian Menu

Starters

  • Samosa Chaat - Crispy potato samosas smashed and topped with tangy tamarind chutney, mint sauce, and yogurt
  • Paneer Tikka - Marinated cottage cheese cubes grilled with bell peppers and onions in a clay oven
  • Chick - Minced chicken seasoned with aromatic spices and herbs, skewered and roasted

Main Courses

  • Butter Chicken - Tender chicken pieces simmered in a rich, creamy tomato and butter gravy
  • Paneer Butter Masala - Soft cottage cheese cubes cooked in a rich, spiced tomato and butter sauce
  • Dal Makhani - Whole black lentils and red kidney beans slow-cooked overnight with butter and cream
  • Hyderabadi Biryani - Fragrant basmati rice layered with spiced tender chicken, saffron, and aromatic herbs

Breads & Sides

  • Garlic Naan - Traditional leavened flatbread baked in a clay oven and topped with fresh garlic and cilantro
  • Butter Roti - Whole wheat flatbread brushed with a touch of butter
  • Jeera Rice - Basmati rice tempered with cumin seeds and whole spices

Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun - Deep-fried milk solids dumplings soaked in a warm, fragrant rose and cardamom sugar syrup
  • Mango Kulfi - Traditional dense and creamy Indian ice cream flavored with fresh mango pulp
  • Rasmalai - Soft paneer discs immersed in chilled, sweetened, and thickened milk flavored with cardamom and pistachios

Beverages

  • Mango Lassi - A refreshing, blended yogurt drink mixed with sweet mango pulp
  • Masala Chai - Indian spiced tea brewed with fresh ginger, cardamom, and whole spices
  • Nimbu Paani - Traditional Indian lemonade spiced with roasted cumin and black salt
