< Streams and Subjects
  1. Science Stream
    • Physics

      The study of matter, energy, motion, and the fundamental forces of nature.

    • Chemistry

      The study of the composition, structure, properties, and reactions of matter.

    • Mathematics

      The study of numbers, quantities, shapes, and patterns.

  2. Commerce Stream
    • Accountancy

      The systematic recording, measuring, and communication of financial transactions.

    • Business Studies

      The study of management principles, organizational structures, and business operations.

    • Economics

      The study of how societies allocate scarce resources to produce and consume goods and services.

  3. Arts
    • History

      The critical study of past events, civilizations, and the evolution of human societies over time.

    • Political Science

      The study of political systems, governments, public policies, and international relations.

    • Geography

      The study of Earth's landscapes, environments, populations, and spatial relationships.
